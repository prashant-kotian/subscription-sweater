# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project
and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to creating a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and life experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, trolling, or inflammatory jokes
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (e.g. an account, credentials, or a
  personal address) without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces
when an individual is representing the project. It also applies when
submissions (code, issues, PRs) involve the project's infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated and will receive a response deemed appropriate to the
circumstances. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with
regard to the reporter of an incident.
